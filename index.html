Worth The Scroll

A content publishing application for preparing, managing and publishing short-form video content.

About Worth The Scroll

Worth The Scroll is a content publishing application used to prepare, manage and publish short-form video content to supported social media platforms.

The application provides tools for managing the content publishing workflow, from preparing videos and publication metadata through to publishing content to connected social media accounts.

Features

Worth The Scroll provides tools for managing short-form video content, preparing titles and other publication metadata, and publishing approved content to supported social media platforms.

Content is reviewed and prepared before being submitted for publication.

TikTok Integration

Worth The Scroll integrates with TikTok to allow an authorized TikTok user to connect their TikTok account and publish video content through the application.

TikTok account access is used only for functionality explicitly authorized by the user. Worth The Scroll does not publish content to a TikTok account without the account owner's authorization.

Account Authorization

Users connecting a TikTok account are redirected to TikTok's authorization process. The user chooses whether to grant Worth The Scroll the permissions required to provide the requested publishing functionality.
